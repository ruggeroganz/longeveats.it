Cookie policy

How this site uses cookies and similar technologies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a site saves in your browser. They can be used to make a site work, to remember choices you have made, or to follow what you do across different sites.

What this site uses

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies of its own, uses no analytics and no advertising or profiling tools. Your browser may still store technical data needed to load the pages, which does not identify you.

Third parties

If you follow a link to Instagram or Facebook, those sites may set their own cookies once you are on them. Their use of cookies is described in their own policies and is outside our control.

Managing cookies

You can delete cookies already saved and block new ones from your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not affect the way this site works.
