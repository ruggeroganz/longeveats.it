About us

“We are what we eat.”

Ludwig Feuerbach

We live longer, but not better

In Europe life expectancy keeps rising, but the years lived in good health do not. Stress, chronic inflammation, poor eating and broken sleep are wearing down quality of life, often in the people who push hardest: founders, managers and urban professionals.

Meanwhile health tech has exploded. Smart rings and tracking apps collect huge amounts of data, yet it rarely turns into something you can act on every day. People know how they slept and how many steps they took, but not what to eat today to live better tomorrow.

LONGEVEATS was born to close that gap.

Food first, connected to the science of longevity

We start from the one habit everyone repeats every day, eating, and connect it to longevity technology. LONGEVEATS brings together our cafés, functional catering and an app that turns personal data into a daily nutrition experience through the LONGEVEATS Score.

We are not just building a food brand. We are building the nutrition infrastructure of the longevity economy.

Vision

To become the point of reference for longevity nutrition, turning everyday food into a real tool to live longer and better.

Mission

To build an ecosystem that brings together functional nutrition, technology and biometric data, offering personalised meals and solutions that improve health, energy and performance.
